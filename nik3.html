Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding admissions, fees, hostel, bus routes, and scholarships.

How do I apply for admission at HNU? +
You can submit the Online Admission Enquiry form on this website or visit the Admissions Directorate at our Ramanathapuram campus. Our team will verify your eligibility and guide you through document submission and enrollment.
Are merit scholarships available for students? +
Yes! HNU offers merit scholarships granting up to 100% tuition fee waiver for students with outstanding marks in 10+2 / Diploma examinations, as well as sports achievements and economically weaker sections.
Are hostel facilities available for both boys and girls? +
Yes, we offer separate, fully guarded residential hostels for boys and girls with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric security, RO purified water, Wi-Fi, study halls, and nutritious multi-cuisine dining.
What areas are covered by the university bus service? +
University buses ply across major routes covering Ramanathapuram town, Paramakudi, Kilakarai, Mandapam, Rameswaram, Sayalgudi, and surrounding suburban stops with tracking and safety supervisors.
Can I pay university fees in installments? +
Yes, the university allows term-wise and semester-wise installment options. Education loan support documentation is also issued to students wishing to apply for bank loans.
